FCA research note

Discovery

What five years of my own portfolio taught me

I invest across several Italian brokers since 2021 and I'm exactly the target user. One user is a hypothesis, not proof, so the next step is 6-8 interviews.

Hype buys cluster around news

My worst entries came from social feeds and big events, not from my plan.

So: ask "why now?" right before the buy.

Concentration hides in plain sight

One "small" stock plus overlapping thematic ETFs meant far more exposure to one theme than I chose.

So: show the real weight, ETFs included.

The cost is real

About 1.4 points a year behind the MSCI World, with deeper drawdowns. Thematic bets and idle cash explain most of it.

So: protect the plan, not the trade count.

Friction works when I choose it

Turning off notifications helped more than willpower. No app I use lets me add a pause on purpose.

So: make it a setting, never a wall.

The difference: Binance has a cooling-off that locks futures trading for a set time. It's a brake for the whole account. Reflect Mode is a pause for one order, chosen by the user, with their own exposure on screen and zero friction on plans.
