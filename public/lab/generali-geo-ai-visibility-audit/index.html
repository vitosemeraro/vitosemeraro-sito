What it means

Six findings, six moves

The Lifetime Partner gap

Life-event answers with Generali as a real option2/8

Generali's strategy is built on life events. That's exactly where the answers send people elsewhere.

→ Answer-first content for each life event

The group doesn't add up

Neutral answers naming Genertel that link it to Generali0/5

Genertel appears, but the brand family is invisible to the engines.

→ Machine-readable brand family (markup, Wikidata)

Other people tell the story

Answers citing generali.com2/16

The engines quote comparison sites, Trustpilot and competitors' guides. On mortgages, Groupama's guide is the source.

→ Publish the page the engine wants to cite

Reviews decide reputation

Trustpilot score quoted by Google1/5

The branded answer is fair on solidity, but its cons come from reviews: price and slow claims.

→ Claims-experience and review programme

Engines disagree on facts

Perplexitydigital tools "weaker"vsGoogledigital tools "advanced"

Both can't be right, and nobody is correcting either.

→ Track accuracy as a KPI, fix the source pages

Price questions are lost

Allianz Direct"from €124"vsGeneralinot named

On "cheapest car cover" the online insurers win by default.

→ Route price intent to Genertel, signed as Generali

Agentic pre-sales

From a life event to the right product or advisor

The engines already run this conversation, without Generali. The same flow can live on Generali's own channels.

1 · Listen

Life event

"We just had a baby." No jargon needed.

2 · Understand

3-5 questions

Income, debts, dependants. No sales pressure.

3 · Explain

Grounded

Every claim cites the product documents. Doubt → a person.

4 · Route

Product or agent

Simple → online quote. Complex → agent, context filled in.

We just had our second child and we have a mortgage. Do we need life insurance?
Many families in your situation choose term life cover for both parents, sized on the mortgage plus a few years of expenses. Two quick questions to give you a range: how much is left on the mortgage, and do you both have an income?Illustrative tone. Sources on each claim, advisor one tap away.
