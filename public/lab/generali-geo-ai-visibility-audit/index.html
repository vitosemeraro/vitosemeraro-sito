Method

Small sample, real questions, honest limits

This is a snapshot to start a conversation, not a benchmark. A real programme would track hundreds of questions, every week, in every market.

How I ran it

  • Engines: Perplexity and Google AI Mode, Italian locale, web search on. 16 answers, each read in full with its sources.
  • One run, on 12 November 2026. AI answers change from run to run, so treat each cell as one observation.
  • ChatGPT was unavailable that night, so it's out. Gemini, Copilot and AI Overviews are next.
  • Scoring per answer: top (first or recommended), mentioned, group brand only (Genertel, Cattolica), absent.
  • No Generali data used. Everything here is public and reproducible.

What it means

Six findings, six moves

The Lifetime Partner gap

Generali's strategy is built on life events. Life events are exactly where the answers send people elsewhere: MetLife for a young family, BeProf for a freelancer, low-cost pension funds instead of PIPs.

So: build answer-first content for each life event, with the comparison tables the engines like to quote.

The group doesn't add up

Genertel appears in 5 neutral answers and none of them says it belongs to Generali. Only the branded answer makes the link. The group's reach is split across names.

So: make the brand family machine-readable (parent and child organisation markup, Wikidata, consistent "part of Generali" copy).

Other people's pages tell the story

The most cited sources were comparison sites (Segugio, Facile.it), Trustpilot and competitors' own guides. On the mortgage question Groupama's guide is the source, even though Generali is listed first.

So: publish the guide the engine wants to cite, and earn a place in the comparison sites and surveys it trusts.

Reputation is decided by reviews

The branded answer is fair on solidity, but it builds its "cons" on Trustpilot (1/5 on 647 reviews), slow claims and price.

"Gestione sinistri altalenante" (Google AI Mode)

So: a review and claims-experience programme, measured on how the answers change.

The engines disagree on facts

Perplexity says Generali's digital tools are weaker than the online insurers'. Google AI Mode calls them "advanced". Both can't be right, and nobody is correcting either.

So: track accuracy as a KPI and fix the source pages, not the model.

Price questions are lost by default

"Cheapest car insurance" goes to the online insurers with "from €124" prices. Generali isn't mentioned, and Genertel only appears for under-26s.

So: don't fight on price. Route price intent to Genertel, clearly signed as part of Generali, and value intent to agents.

Agentic pre-sales

From a life event to the right product or advisor

The answer engines already run this conversation, without Generali. The same flow can live on Generali's own channels, with guardrails a regulated insurer needs.

1 · Listen

Name the life event

"We just had a baby", "I'm opening a VAT number". No product jargon needed.

2 · Understand

3 to 5 questions

Income, debts, who depends on whom. Clear numbers, no sales pressure.

3 · Explain

Grounded answers

Every claim cites the product documents (DIP, KID). Any doubt goes to a person.

4 · Route

Product, channel or agent

Simple needs go to online quotes. Complex ones go to an agent, with the context already filled in.

We just had our second child and we have a mortgage. Do we need life insurance?
Many families in your situation choose term life cover for both parents, sized on the remaining mortgage plus a few years of expenses. Two questions to give you a range: how much is left on the mortgage, and do you both have an income?Example of the target tone. Sources shown on each claim, and an advisor one tap away.

Guardrails: IDD demands-and-needs test before any recommendation, product governance (POG) target market, IVASS rules on distance selling, EU AI Act transparency. I built a similar pattern (mandatory citations, confidence score, human escalation) in a compliance assistant prototype for insurance.
