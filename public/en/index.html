How I work

Four phases. AI agents do the work, I approve every step.

01

Discover and design

I start from people, not technology. I use design thinking to understand the problem and Claude Design to turn an idea into a prototype that users can try within a few hours.

Agent
Dedicated skills and Claude Design
The PM approves
the problem and the prototype, after users have tried it

02

Build with agents

Vibe coding, yes, but governed. With Claude Code, Skills and MCP I take an idea all the way to a working product: login, backend, data, costs under control. That way I validate in days and speak the same language as technical teams.

Agent
Claude Code
The PM approves
every step the agent takes

03

Test and document

An AI that makes mistakes loses users’ trust. I automate the test suite with agents and Playwright, and I generate business and functional analyses that I then check line by line.

Agent
QA agent, AI-generated analyses
The PM approves
the quality of the outputs, every analysis and every ticket

04

Drive adoption

Technology is the easy part. Training on real work, a community of “champion” colleagues, adoption KPIs collected without monitoring people. With a certified method (PMI-CPMAI).

03

Playbook

The method behind the projects: what I’ve learned taking products and AI into production.

  1. QualityThe PO leads, the agent executes: automating testingThe product owner becomes the director of automated testing: they own the domain knowledge and the decisions, while an AI agent acts as the technical driver.
  2. AdoptionBringing an AI tool into a teamTechnology is the easy part. The hard part is making people feel the tool works for them.
  3. DiscoveryFrom research to roadmap: hypotheses, not opinionsEvery design choice becomes a hypothesis that can be disproved.
